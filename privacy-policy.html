Vertical_Cut

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how White Bun handles information when you use the Vertical_Cut mobile game. The game is a precision puzzle game where players clear round balls by timing vertical cuts.

Who We Are

Vertical_Cut is provided by White Bun. For privacy questions, contact us at dev.oniksaha@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

Vertical_Cut does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or other personal profile information.

The game may save gameplay information locally on your device, such as completed levels, best scores, best cut counts, minimum cut counts, unlocked levels, and completion timestamps. This information is used only to restore your progress and show your scores inside the game.

On Android, Vertical_Cut uses third-party advertising services to show ads. These advertising services may automatically collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, device information, and related identifiers. This information is used to deliver ads, limit ad repetition, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with legal and platform requirements.

How We Use Information

Locally saved gameplay information is used to:

  • Resume your progress when you reopen the game.
  • Show completed levels, unlocked levels, scores, and replay results.
  • Compare your cuts against the minimum cut count for each level.

Advertising information on Android may be used to:

  • Show ads inside the Android version of the game.
  • Measure ad delivery and performance.
  • Detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, and technical problems.
  • Comply with app store, advertising, and legal requirements.

Ads, Analytics, and Third-Party Services

The Android version of Vertical_Cut includes ads served by third-party advertising services, such as Google AdMob if integrated in the app. These services may use device identifiers and other data described in this Privacy Policy to provide personalized or non-personalized ads, depending on your region, device settings, consent choices, and the advertising service's policies.

Vertical_Cut does not currently include social login, online leaderboards, cloud saves, in-app purchases, or remote audio downloads. Sound effects are bundled with the app and played locally.

You can learn more about how Google uses information for advertising at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. On Android, information collected by advertising services may be shared with those services and their partners for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, security, and compliance purposes.

Children's Privacy

Vertical_Cut is intended for a general audience. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and delete it if applicable.

Data Storage and Deletion

Gameplay progress is stored locally on your device. You can delete locally stored game data by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

Advertising data handled by third-party advertising services is controlled according to those services' policies and your device settings. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID and manage ad personalization in your device privacy settings where supported.

Security

We use reasonable measures to keep the game safe. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game changes or when legal, platform, or operational requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page shows when the policy was last revised.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the Vertical_Cut game, contact White Bun at dev.oniksaha@gmail.com.
