Vertical_Cut

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how White Bun handles information when you use the Vertical_Cut mobile game. The game is a precision puzzle game where players clear round balls by timing vertical cuts.

Who We Are

Vertical_Cut is provided by White Bun. For privacy questions, contact us at dev.oniksaha@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

Vertical_Cut does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or other personal profile information.

The game may save gameplay information locally on your device, such as completed levels, best scores, best cut counts, minimum cut counts, unlocked levels, and completion timestamps. This information is used only to restore your progress and show your scores inside the game.

How We Use Information

Locally saved gameplay information is used to:

  • Resume your progress when you reopen the game.
  • Show completed levels, unlocked levels, scores, and replay results.
  • Compare your cuts against the minimum cut count for each level.

Ads, Analytics, and Third-Party Services

Vertical_Cut does not currently include advertising networks, analytics services, social login, online leaderboards, cloud saves, in-app purchases, or remote audio downloads. Sound effects are bundled with the app and played locally.

If these features are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated before the updated version of the game is released.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Because Vertical_Cut does not currently collect personal information through the game, we do not share personal information with advertisers, analytics providers, or data brokers.

Children's Privacy

Vertical_Cut is intended for a general audience. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and delete it if applicable.

Data Storage and Deletion

Gameplay progress is stored locally on your device. You can delete locally stored game data by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

Security

We use reasonable measures to keep the game safe. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game changes or when legal, platform, or operational requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page shows when the policy was last revised.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the Vertical_Cut game, contact White Bun at dev.oniksaha@gmail.com.
